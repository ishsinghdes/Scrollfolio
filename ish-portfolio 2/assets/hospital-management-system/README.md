# Hospital Management System · UI/UX

Eight authentic screenshots (four desktop, four mobile) and four silent, four-second looping recordings. Captured from Ish Singh’s Aspatal HTML/CSS/JavaScript and Three.js prototype. Dark mode is the primary presentation; light-mode bed management and sign-in provide contrast.

All people, patient records, hospital readings and stock are synthetic. These are interface studies, not clinical results or evidence of a deployed hospital system.

`mobile/` contains lightweight gallery variants; `posters/` contains video previews. Screenshot originals are 2× resolution. MP4 files use H.264, 30 fps, fast-start metadata and no audio. Captions and dimensions are in `manifest.json`.

Designed with love, designed with a heart, by Ish.
